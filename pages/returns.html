The 30-Day Promise

Returns & Refunds

Wear it, live with it, be certain of it — and if a piece does not feel like yours, we take it back.

The promise

You have 30 days from delivery to return any piece for a full refund — no questions asked. Collection is arranged and paid for by the maison.

How to return

  • Find the order under My Account and note its reference (e.g. OP-ABC123).
  • Contact the atelier with the reference — we schedule an insured collection within 2 working days.
  • Your refund is released to the original payment method as soon as the piece passes its return inspection, typically within 5 working days.

Conditions

  • Pieces must return in their gift box with the certificate of authenticity.
  • Inscribed pieces can be exchanged for size or fault, but not refunded for change of mind — engraving is permanent and personal.
  • If a stone is ever found to be misrepresented, the 30-day window does not apply: you are refunded in full, whenever you discover it.
Demonstration site: returns are simulated. The Consumer Protection Act still shapes this policy's design.
