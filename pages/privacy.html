POPIA & Your Data

Privacy Policy

What we keep, where it lives, and the rights the Protection of Personal Information Act gives you.

What we collect

  • Account — first name, last name, email address, and a one-way hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • Orders — the pieces you buy, delivery address, and order references. Card numbers are never stored.
  • Preferences — your wishlist, bag, reviews and thumbs votes.

Where it lives

In this demonstration everything above is stored only in your own browser (localStorage) — nothing leaves your device and no third party receives it. When the production back-end launches, this data will move to a secured database under the same principles.

Your POPIA rights

  • Access — see your information at any time under My Account.
  • Correction — update details by contacting the atelier.
  • Deletion — clearing your browser data removes every trace immediately; in production, a deletion request does the same.
  • Objection — we send no marketing without consent, and never sell personal information.

Cookies

The site sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. The only storage used is the functional localStorage described above.
