Frequently Asked

How long does delivery take?

Insured, tracked delivery is free on every order: 2–3 working days to main centres and 3–5 working days elsewhere in South Africa. You'll receive the tracking reference with your invoice.

Are the stones genuine?

Every piece is verified in the atelier before dispatch and travels with its own certificate of authenticity. If any stone ever proves misrepresented, the maison refunds you in full.

Can I return a piece?

Yes — you have 30 days to live with a piece. If it doesn't feel like yours, we arrange collection and refund you in full. Inscribed pieces can be exchanged unless the inscription itself is the issue on our side. See the returns policy.

Can I change or engrave a piece?

Most pieces can be customised in the Studio — pattern, removable elements, an added pendant on necklaces, and a hand-engraved inscription of up to 30 characters. Signature pieces accept inscription only, and the stone is never changed.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Card and Instant EFT at our online checkout. Prices include 15% VAT, and instalment options are shown on each piece.

How do I know my ring or chain size?

Our size & care guide shows chain lengths on the body and how to measure at home in under a minute.
