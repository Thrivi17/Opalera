Every order travels fully insured, tracked, and beautifully boxed — at no charge.

How it works

  • Your order is inspected and certified in the atelier, then sealed in the maison's signature gift box.
  • Every parcel is insured for its full value and requires a signature on arrival.
  • Your tracking reference accompanies your invoice, and you can view every order under My Account.

Gifts

Choose gift wrapping at checkout (complimentary) and add a hand-written card message of up to 160 characters. Invoices for gifted orders never show prices in the box — the paperwork travels separately to you.
