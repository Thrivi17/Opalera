Size & Care Guide

How each length sits, how to measure at home in a minute, and how to keep your pieces bright for a lifetime.

Necklace & pendant chain lengths

Every OPALÉRA necklace and pendant ships on an adjustable 38–41 cm chain, and the guide below shows how common lengths sit so you can picture yours.

LengthNameWhere it sits
36–38 cmCollarSnug against the neck — high necklines, formal wear
38–41 cmChoker / Princess (OPALÉRA standard)At or just below the collarbone — the classic pendant position
43–48 cmPrincess longOver the collarbone, ideal above crew necks
50–60 cmMatinéeBetween collarbone and bust — layering length

Measuring at home

  • Take a shoelace or ribbon, drape it where you want the piece to sit, and mark where it meets.
  • Lay it flat against a ruler — that's your length. Our adjustable chain covers 38–41 cm, so anything in that window fits perfectly.
  • For earrings, no sizing is needed — every pair uses a standard post or hook.

Caring for your pieces

  • Gold & white gold — wipe with the soft cloth in your gift box after wearing; store pieces separately so chains don't scratch each other.
  • Pearls — last on, first off: perfume and hairspray before, pearls after. Wipe with a barely damp cloth; never soak.
  • Stones — a soft brush with lukewarm water and a drop of mild soap restores fire; dry fully before storing.
  • Inscriptions — engraved surfaces clean the same way; avoid abrasive polish over the engraving.
