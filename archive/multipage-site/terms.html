Terms & Conditions

The agreement between you and the maison, in plain language.

1. Who we are

OPALÉRA is a student demonstration storefront built for IFM2B10 by Quantum Coders. It simulates a South African fine-jewellery e-commerce business; no real goods are sold and no real payments are processed.

2. Orders & payment

  • All prices are shown in South African Rand and include VAT at 15%.
  • An order exists once checkout completes and an order reference (OP-…) is issued with its invoice.
  • Promotional codes apply to the items total before VAT presentation and cannot be combined.

3. Customisation & inscriptions

  • Customisation never changes a piece's stone. Signature pieces accept inscription only.
  • Inscriptions are limited to 30 characters and screened for offensive content; the maison may decline an engraving.

4. Accounts

You are responsible for your account credentials. We store your name, email and a one-way password hash — see the privacy policy.

5. Returns

The 30-day return promise applies as described in Returns & Refunds.

6. Liability

As a demonstration project, the site is provided as-is for academic assessment. Nothing on it constitutes a real offer of sale.
